# Code of Conduct

## Expected conduct

- Discuss ideas and defects with specific evidence.
- Critique artifacts and decisions rather than contributors.
- Respect privacy, authorship, platform terms, and content boundaries.
- Disclose copied material, generated fixtures, conflicts of interest, and test limitations.
- Accept maintainers' requests to narrow, edit, or remove disruptive contributions.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, plagiarism, deliberate evidence fabrication, and repeated disruption are not accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct or security-sensitive concerns through the private contact described in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or ban participation in proportion to severity. Good-faith reports will be handled privately and without retaliation.
